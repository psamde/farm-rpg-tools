How the comparison works

Extra exploring stays within the suggested locations. The search favors broad material use, penalizes exploring and new locations, without a percentage cap on extra explores. It considers all recipes, then checks up to 4,000 candidate plans with up to 12 crafts each. The search values the amount and share of original supplies consumed, not the number of tiny crafts added. Compare the balanced suggestion with a higher-use alternative when available.
